Salary Analysis
Average Salary by Job Title
USD · Annual average per occupation in the dataset

Graphic Designers top the salary table at $96,423 — a surprising lead given their medium-to-high automation exposure. Lawyers ($92,442) and Chefs ($91,408) follow. Truck Drivers ($84,520) sit lowest, facing both the lowest pay and very high automation risk — a double vulnerability. The salary spread across all jobs is relatively narrow (~$12K), suggesting compensation may not fully price in future disruption risk yet.

Salary vs Automation Risk — Bubble View
Each bubble = 1 job title · Size = number of records · Color = risk tier

No strong positive correlation between salary and safety. High-paying jobs can still be high-risk (e.g. Graphic Designer), while some lower-paid jobs like Nurses are very protected. This challenges the assumption that earning more guarantees job security against AI.

Avg. Salary by Risk Category
Do safer jobs pay more?

Counterintuitively, Medium-risk jobs pay slightly the most ($90,451) and High-risk jobs the least ($87,359). The difference is only ~$3K — salary alone is a poor predictor of automation risk.

Education & Experience
Risk Distribution by Education Level
Stacked count of High / Medium / Low risk per degree

Higher education doesn't dramatically lower risk — PhDs and High School graduates both show very similar High/Low risk ratios (~23%). The strongest protection comes from job type, not degree level. Medium-risk dominates every education tier.

Automation Probability by Experience Level
Does more experience = lower automation risk?

Workers with 11–15 years' experience see the lowest automation risk (48.2%), forming a sweet-spot "mid-career shield." Very early-career workers (0–5 yrs) face the highest risk at 52%. Experience provides some buffer, but it's not a reliable shield alone — job category matters more.

AI Exposure & Tech Growth
AI Exposure Index by Risk Category
How exposed each risk group is to AI tools today

All three risk tiers show nearly identical AI exposure (~0.50) — meaning current AI exposure is not what drives the risk rating. The risk is driven by the nature of tasks, not how much AI workers already use in their jobs today.

Tech Growth Factor by Risk Category
Rate of technological change impacting each risk group

Medium-risk jobs experience the highest tech growth (1.0003), suggesting they're at an inflection point — currently manageable, but rapid tech change could push them into High-risk territory by 2030. Low-risk jobs see the slowest growth (0.981), indicating greater structural stability.
