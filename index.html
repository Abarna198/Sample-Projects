Key Insights
Insights · What the Data Really Tells Us
6 unique angles extracted from 1,000 user records
⏰
Heavy Usage Dominates
38.8% of users spend over 300 minutes (5 hours) daily on social media — far beyond healthy usage benchmarks of ~60 min/day.
🏆
Facebook Wins Engagement
Facebook scores the highest engagement ratio at 12.86 likes+follows per post — despite lower avg session time vs Instagram and Twitter.
⚡
TikTok Paradox
TikTok users spend the least time per day (233 min avg) yet post the most (9.6/day avg), suggesting short, high-frequency content creation.
📸
Instagram Posts Most
Instagram leads with 11.3 avg posts/day and 264 min session time — indicating deeply habitual, content-heavy users.
🔗
LinkedIn Punches Above
LinkedIn has the most users (147) and maintains strong engagement (12.0 score) despite typically lower engagement expectations for a professional platform.
📉
Twitter Likes Deficit
Twitter has the lowest likes/post ratio (8.44) among all platforms — content gets less positive reinforcement despite high session times of 257 min/day.
